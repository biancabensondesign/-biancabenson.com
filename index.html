About

From reading a room to reading a codebase

The short version of a longer story.

My training in graphic design at the KABK, and subsequent training in web development, gave me the foundations, but some of what shaped my eye for good UX didn’t happen at a desk. As a museum guide at Voorlinden and the Clingenbosch Sculpture Garden, and in gallery sales at Carré d'Artistes, my job was to read what held someone's attention and what lost it - in real time, with no undo button.

That instinct carried straight into frontend work: CMS migrations, continuous iteration on live sites, interactive kiosk experiences all the way down to simple Wordpress sites. The medium changed from a room to a screen, but the question stayed the same - does this actually work for the person in front of it?

Verosol - Dealer ordering

Shopping cart overview: concept, UX/UI & build

Visit site ↗

Verosol's dealer environment is where partners order stock. Dealers needed a clearer way to review what they were ordering, so I proposed a dedicated cart overview, designed how it should look and behave, and built it. The goal was that dealers could check, adjust and confirm an order at a glance before placing it. It went from noticing the gap to shipping the solution.

UX/UI design Concept to code B2B ordering

Delivered via HappyFlow as a team project - the cart overview was my own design and build.
