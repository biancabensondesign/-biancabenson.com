About

From reading a room to reading a codebase

The short version of a longer story.

My training in graphic design at KABK, and subsequent training in web development, gave me the foundations, but some of what shaped my eye for good UX didn’t happen at a desk. As a museum guide at Voorlinden and the Clingenbosch Sculpture Garden, and in gallery sales at Carré d'Artistes, my job was to read what held someone's attention and what lost it — in real time, with no undo button.

That instinct carried straight into frontend work: CMS migrations, continuous iteration on live sites, interactive kiosk experiences. The medium changed from a room to a screen, but the question stayed the same — does this actually work for the person in front of it?

Frontend development Creative direction CMS-based systems Team collaboration Graphic design AI forward Productivity software Project management software

Off the clock: yoga, cooking from scratch, hiking, reading and a running fascination with the (night) sky - aurora chasing, JWST updates, solar- and lunar eclipses and whatever's visible in a dark-sky window. I write about various topics on- and offline.
